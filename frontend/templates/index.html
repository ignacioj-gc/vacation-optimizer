Vacation Optimizer

Generates optimal vacation calendars maximizing time off work accounting for public holidays.

If necessary, select a country from the dropdown menu to view holidays. Select any non-holiday to add it to the calculation, or select holidays to remove them from consideration. Use the Vacation Days field to enter the amount of vacation days available for use. Once ready, click Optimize to generate a vacation calendar.
